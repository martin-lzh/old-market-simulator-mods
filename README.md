# Old Market Simulator Mods

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

A collection of unofficial Mods to make running your Old Market Simulator shop a little easier. Pick the features you want; each Mod has its own download and instructions.

### Choose a Mod

| Mod | What it adds | Download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Material Cost](material-cost-mod/README.md) | Material costs and estimated profit in reports, recipes and orders | [0.5.1](https://github.com/martin-lzh/old-market-simulator-mods/releases/tag/material-cost-v0.5.1) |
| [Coordinates](coordinates-mod/README.md) | Your XYZ position below the money bar; F8 to show/hide | [0.1.3](https://github.com/martin-lzh/old-market-simulator-mods/releases/tag/coordinates-v0.1.3) |
| [Checkout All](checkout-all-mod/README.md) | Hold E or toggle F9 to process products and collect payment | [0.1.4](https://github.com/martin-lzh/old-market-simulator-mods/releases/tag/checkout-all-v0.1.4) |
| [Stack All](stack-all-mod/README.md) | Up to 64 containers per slot, plus repeated drop/throw | [0.2.1](https://github.com/martin-lzh/old-market-simulator-mods/releases/tag/stack-all-v0.2.1) |
| [Price Probability](price-probability-mod/README.md) | Price acceptance preview and automatic pricing rules | [0.1.1](https://github.com/martin-lzh/old-market-simulator-mods/releases/tag/price-probability-v0.1.1) |
| [Navigation](navigation-mod/README.md) | Minimap, large map, compass and personal markers | 0.2.0 being prepared; no public download yet |

The Mod pages explain upcoming changes as well as the version currently available to download. Navigation includes Eastern Town with four central-market states and an Island preview with 16 fixed POIs; Island in-game alignment remains pending.

### Getting started

These Mods are for **Old Market Simulator 2.1.6 on Windows**. Most use **BepInEx 5**; Material Cost also has a **MelonLoader 0.7.3** edition. Choose one loader, not both. Loaders are not included in the Mod downloads.

1. Close the game and back up your save and any old plugin.
2. Open the Mod's page above and follow its installation instructions.
3. Download the Mod ZIP from its release page. GitHub's **Source code** download is not an installable plugin.
4. Start the game and try the controls described in the Mod's guide.

**Using Stack All with friends? Everyone needs the same version.** Before removing it, follow its [save and removal instructions](stack-all-mod/README.md). Other Mods have their own multiplayer notes.

Current downloads are prereleases. Visual and other local in-game testing is complete; multiplayer testing is the only remaining test area. Keep a backup, especially before game updates or changing inventory Mods.

### Help and languages

The interface follows the game's language, including English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese and the other game-supported languages. You do not need a separate language pack. Coordinates uses X/Y/Z labels.

Found a problem or have an idea? Chinese and English are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/martin-lzh/old-market-simulator-mods/issues). Please include the Mod version, what you were doing and what happened. See [Support](SUPPORT.md) for details; avoid posting complete saves or private logs. Security concerns go through [Security reporting](SECURITY.md).

### 开始使用

适用于 **Windows 版 Old Market Simulator 2.1.6**。多数 Mod 使用 **BepInEx 5**；Material Cost 还提供 **MelonLoader 0.7.3** 版本。加载器二选一，不要同时启用，Mod 下载包也不包含加载器。

1. 退出游戏，备份存档和已有的旧插件。
2. 打开上方对应 Mod 页面，按说明安装。
3. 在发布页选择 Mod ZIP，GitHub 的 **Source code** 下载不是可安装插件。
4. 启动游戏，按使用说明试试功能。

**和朋友一起用 Stack All 时，所有人都需要相同版本。**卸载前请按它的[存档与卸载说明](stack-all-mod/README.md)整理库存。其他 Mod 的联机要求见各自页面。

目前下载均为预发布版。视觉及其他本地实机测试已完成，目前只剩多人游戏测试。请留好备份，尤其是在游戏更新或更换库存 Mod 前。
